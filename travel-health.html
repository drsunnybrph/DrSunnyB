Travel Health Consultations

Virtual, one-on-one travel health appointments with a licensed PharmD. Destination risk assessment, vaccine counseling, and OTC travel medication guidance — from your home.

Current scope: Travel health counseling, vaccine recommendations, and OTC medication guidance are within standard CA pharmacist scope of practice and available now. Prescription travel medications (malaria prophylaxis, travel antibiotics, etc.) will be offered once a Collaborative Practice Agreement (CPA) with a supervising physician is finalized — coming soon.

Available now — no CPA required: Destination risk assessment, vaccine clinic referrals & counseling, OTC medication guidance (antidiarrheals, antihistamines, altitude sickness OTC, motion sickness), food & water safety education, and detailed written travel summaries are all within standard CA pharmacist scope of practice.
