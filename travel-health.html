Travel Health Consultations

Virtual, one-on-one travel health appointments with Amrinder Bhandal, PharmD. Destination risk assessment, vaccine counseling, OTC travel medication guidance, and prescription furnishing — serving Solano and Ventura County, California.

Consultation and Medication Furnishing Only. No on-site vaccinations administered. Professional referrals provided for all required immunizations at a clinic near you.
Current scope: Travel health counseling, vaccine referrals, and OTC guidance available now. Prescription travel medications (malaria prophylaxis, travel antibiotics, etc.) will be offered under BPC § 4052.9 once CPA with supervising physician is finalized — coming soon.
Consultations in English · Punjabi · ਪੰਜਾਬੀ · Hindi · हिन्दी

Available now — no CPA required: Destination risk assessment, vaccine clinic referrals & counseling, OTC medication guidance (antidiarrheals, antihistamines, altitude sickness OTC, motion sickness), food & water safety education, and detailed written travel summaries. Especially experienced with South Asia travel corridors (India, Pakistan) — consultations available in English, Punjabi, and Hindi.

Prescription Furnishing — Coming Soon

Under California Business and Professions Code § 4052.9, Dr. Sunny B Pharmacist Consulting will furnish prescription travel medications directly to patients once our Collaborative Practice Agreement (CPA) is finalized. Join the waitlist to be notified immediately when this launches.

  • Malaria prophylaxis (Atovaquone-Proguanil, Doxycycline, Mefloquine)
  • Traveler's Diarrhea standby antibiotics (Azithromycin, Rifaximin)
  • Altitude sickness (Acetazolamide)
  • Motion sickness (Scopolamine patch)
  • Hepatitis A & Typhoid referral coordination
