The Story

Dr. Sunny B, PharmD

Dr. Sunny B is a licensed emergency medicine pharmacist based in California, operating at the intersection of clinical care and digital strategy. With a Doctor of Pharmacy degree and years of frontline clinical experience, Dr. Sunny B brings doctorate-level expertise directly to patients and small businesses.

The Dr. Sunny B brand covers virtual travel health consultations, evidence-based OTC and supplement counseling, and web consulting for small businesses — built around accessibility, trust, and results.

All clinical services are provided within California Board of Pharmacy scope of practice guidelines, with a Collaborative Practice Agreement in place for expanded clinical services.
