The Story

Amrinder Bhandal, PharmD

Specialist in Clinical Strategy & Healthcare Operations

Dr. Sunny B is the practice of Amrinder Bhandal, PharmD — a licensed emergency medicine pharmacist based in California, operating at the intersection of clinical care and digital strategy. With a Doctor of Pharmacy degree and years of frontline emergency medicine experience, Dr. Bhandal brings doctorate-level expertise directly to patients and small businesses.

The practice serves patients across Solano County (Fairfield, Vacaville, Suisun City) and Ventura County (Simi Valley, Oxnard, Thousand Oaks), with multilingual consultations available in English, Punjabi, and Hindi — serving the South Asian diaspora and VFR (Visiting Friends & Relatives) travel market with culturally competent care.

All clinical services are provided within California Board of Pharmacy scope of practice guidelines (BPC § 4052.9). A Collaborative Practice Agreement (CPA) with a supervising physician is currently being finalized — prescription furnishing services will be available once in place. We utilize automated electronic physician notification systems to fulfill mandatory 14-day regulatory reporting required under California law.
