EMR Recommendations

Best EMR for a Solo Telehealth Pharmacist

Ranked by cost, telehealth capability, intake forms, and ease of solo setup.

Top Pick
Jane App
~$54/month solo
  • Built-in telehealth (HD video)
  • Online scheduling + reminders
  • Custom intake forms
  • HIPAA-compliant portal
  • Invoicing + payments
Best Free Option
Charm EHR
Free (solo) / $25+/mo
  • Free solo practice tier
  • Telehealth built in
  • Intake forms + e-signatures
  • Patient portal
  • HIPAA compliant
Practice Fusion
$199/month
  • ONC-certified EHR
  • Telehealth add-on
  • E-prescribing
  • Best for CPA prescribing
DrChrono
~$199–499/month
  • iOS-first, great on iPhone
  • Telehealth built in
  • Custom intake forms
  • E-prescribing
Cal.com + Doxy.me
Free combo
  • Cal.com: free scheduling
  • Doxy.me: free HIPAA video
  • Google Forms for intake
  • Zero-cost startup option
OptiMantra
~$99/month
  • Best for wellness/functional
  • Telehealth + scheduling
  • Lab integration
  • Good supplement workflow

Recommendation: Start with Cal.com + Doxy.me + Google Forms (free) while early-stage. Once you hit 10+ monthly patients, upgrade to Jane App (~$54/mo) for the all-in-one experience — telehealth, scheduling, intake, invoicing, and portal.

CPA Resources

Setting Up Your CPA

CA Pharmacist CPA

Under California B&P Code, pharmacists with CPAs can initiate/adjust drug therapy, order labs, and furnish travel medications under standardized procedures.

Finding a Collaborating Physician

Connect through Doximity, medical groups, or former clinical rotation contacts. The CPA defines your scope — negotiate what services you'll provide.

Legal & Compliance

Your CPA must comply with CA BOP regulations and cannot violate Stark Law or Anti-Kickback Statute. Consult a healthcare attorney before launching.
