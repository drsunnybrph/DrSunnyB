Scheduling Stack

Platform Decision Map

Cal.com — All Bookings

All services (travel health, supplements, health coaching, web consulting) booked through Cal.com. Free. No HIPAA BAA needed since no PHI collected at booking — that goes to Charm. Patient books → auto-sends Charm intake link.

Charm EHR — Records + Telehealth

All clinical intake forms here. Do not use Google Forms for health info — not HIPAA. Patient completes Charm form before visit. Use Charm's built-in video for the actual appointment.

Charm EHR — Intake & Records Only

After booking on Cal.com, patient receives a Charm EHR link for HIPAA-secure intake. All clinical records stored here. Do NOT collect PHI through Cal.com booking forms.

CPA Resources

Setting Up Your CPA

CA Pharmacist CPA

Under California B&P Code §4052.1, pharmacists with CPAs can initiate/adjust drug therapy, order labs, and furnish travel medications under standardized procedures.

Finding a Collaborating Physician

Connect through Doximity, medical groups, or former clinical rotation contacts. The CPA defines your scope — negotiate what services you'll provide. Travel medicine MDs are ideal.

Legal & Compliance

CPA must comply with CA BOP regulations. Cannot violate Stark Law or Anti-Kickback Statute. Consult a healthcare attorney before launching Rx services. Update site once CPA is signed.

Site update checklist once CPA is secured (BPC § 4052.9): (1) Update travel-health.html disclaimer to confirm CPA is in place. (2) Add "Prescription coordination" back to Comprehensive plan features.
