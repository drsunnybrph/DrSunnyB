Wellness Service

Health Coaching

Fitness goals, lifestyle optimization, and OTC supplement guidance — all backed by a licensed PharmD. No guesswork, no marketing BS. Just evidence-based wellness support.

Health coaching is a wellness education service. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. OTC supplement recommendations are within standard CA pharmacist scope of practice. For prescription medications or medical concerns, please consult your physician.

What makes this different: You're not getting advice from a personal trainer who read a blog. You're getting OTC supplement guidance from a clinical pharmacist — someone who knows what's actually in the bottle, what the evidence shows, and what might interact with your current medications.
