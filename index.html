CPA Resources

Setting Up Your CPA

CA Pharmacist CPA

Under California B&P Code, pharmacists with CPAs can initiate/adjust drug therapy, order labs, and furnish travel medications under standardized procedures.

Finding a Collaborating Physician

Connect through Doximity, medical groups, or former clinical rotation contacts. The CPA defines your scope — negotiate what services you'll provide.

Legal & Compliance

Your CPA must comply with CA BOP regulations and cannot violate Stark Law or Anti-Kickback Statute. Consult a healthcare attorney before launching.
