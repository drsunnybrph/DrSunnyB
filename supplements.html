Clinical Supplement & Interaction Audits

Most people don't know their supplements could be sabotaging their medications — or landing them in the ER. As an Emergency Medicine PharmD, Amrinder Bhandal has seen these interactions firsthand. Now he brings that clinical lens directly to you.

No-Diagnosis Disclaimer: Supplement and OTC counseling is within standard CA pharmacist scope of practice (BPC § 4052.9). This service does not constitute medical diagnosis, treatment of disease, or prescribing. For diagnosed conditions, consult your physician.

Why an ER Pharmacist? Dangerous drug-supplement interactions are a leading cause of preventable ER visits. St. John's Wort with antidepressants. Fish oil with blood thinners. Melatonin with seizure medications. As an Emergency Medicine PharmD, Dr. Bhandal has seen these cases — and screens your stack with the same clinical rigor used in the ER, not just a Google search.
